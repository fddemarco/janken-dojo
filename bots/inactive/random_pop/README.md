# Random population

Research opponents, never in the field: bots sampled from the kit's grammar by
research/tools/random_pop.py (idea #91), seeds 1 and 2, 30 random L2s and 30 random
L3s each. The stronger half of each seed (by margin vs 10 strong field L1-L2s) is the
train list (seed 1, research/gauntlet/randpop_train.txt) and the test list (seed 2,
randpop_test.txt): unknown opponents in numbers, tuned on one and confirmed on the
other.
